Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, including home, office and shop visits. Tell us your area when you book and we will confirm availability.

How much does a service cost?

Pricing depends on the device, the parts needed and the work required. Send us your device model and the issue, and we will discuss the next step with you before any work begins.

Is my booking confirmed straight away?

No. The booking form sends us an appointment request. NESH will contact you to confirm the available slot.

Will my data be safe during an upgrade or transfer?

We handle customer devices and data carefully. For important files, we recommend a backup before any upgrade or repair, and we can help you arrange one.
